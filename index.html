How this resource works

Information changes. Program hours, funding, prices, eligibility and openings can change. Listings will show when they were last checked whenever possible.
Official sources first. Whenever possible, this hub links directly to the organization, school, government program or provider.
Search is clearly labeled. “Search Current Information” may open an external web search or provider page. The basic site does not pretend it searched the internet if it did not.
Education, not professional advice. Money information is educational, legal information is not legal advice, and health information is not medical diagnosis or treatment.
Emergency situations. If someone is in immediate danger or facing a life-threatening emergency, contact 911 or the appropriate emergency/crisis service.
Flourish Anyway cannot guarantee eligibility or funding. This hub helps people find and understand resources; final eligibility and availability are determined by each provider.
