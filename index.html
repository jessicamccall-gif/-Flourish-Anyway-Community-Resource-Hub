Who this hub is for

Flourish Anyway's own programs are centered on women age 18 and older, including peer-led recovery support, wellness and community connection. The Community Resource Hub is broader: it helps women find resources for themselves, their children, their families, their households and other community needs. Some listings are also open to single adults, seniors, veterans, people with disabilities and other Jackson or Josephine County residents. Each provider sets its own eligibility rules.

How this resource works

Jackson and Josephine Counties first. Listings are limited to local providers or Oregon programs that people in Jackson or Josephine County can actually use.
Information changes. Program hours, funding, prices, eligibility and openings can change. Listings will show when they were last checked whenever possible.
Official sources first. Resource listings link directly to the organization, school, government program or provider whenever an official page exists. Flourish Anyway information links back to weflourishanyway.org.
Search is clearly labeled. “Search Current Information” may open an official provider page or an external web search. The site will not claim that information was checked live when it was not.
Education, not professional advice. Money information is educational, legal information is not legal advice, and health information is not medical diagnosis or treatment.
Emergency situations. If someone is in immediate danger or facing a life-threatening emergency, contact 911 or the appropriate emergency/crisis service.
Flourish Anyway cannot guarantee eligibility or funding. This hub helps people find and understand resources; final eligibility, availability and acceptance are determined by each provider.
