<h1>GETTING MY FIRST LICENSE</h1>
<p>Oregon driver's-license steps in plain language. Use this page as a checklist, then confirm current requirements with Oregon DMV.</p></div></header>
<main><div class="wrap">
<section class="step"><h2><span class="num">1</span>Know which license path you are on</h2>
<p>If you have never been licensed, start with Oregon DMV's first-license requirements. If you had a license before and it is suspended or revoked, use the separate <a href="drivers-license.html">Getting My License Back</a> guide.</p></section>

<section class="step"><h2><span class="num">2</span>Gather identity and residency documents</h2>
<p>Before going to DMV, check the current Oregon identity, lawful-status, Social Security and Oregon-residency document rules. REAL ID has additional document requirements.</p>
<div class="ask"><b>Tip:</b> Use DMV's official document checklist before your appointment so you do not make a wasted trip.</div>
<div class="actions"><a href="https://www.oregon.gov/odot/dmv/pages/driverid/index.aspx" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Oregon DMV Driver & ID ↗</a></div></section>

<section class="step"><h2><span class="num">3</span>Study for the knowledge test</h2>
<p>Use the current Oregon Driver Manual and DMV practice information. Focus on signs, right-of-way, safe following distance, speed rules, impaired driving and sharing the road.</p>
<div class="actions"><a href="https://www.oregon.gov/odot/dmv/pages/online_manual/study-section_1.aspx" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Oregon Driver Manual ↗</a></div></section>

<section class="step"><h2><span class="num">4</span>Get your instruction permit if required for your path</h2>
<p>DMV will tell you whether you need a permit and which tests apply based on your age and situation. Ask what you need before paying fees.</p>
<div class="ask"><b>Ask DMV:</b> “I am getting my first Oregon driver’s license. What tests, permit steps, documents and fees apply to me?”</div></section>

<section class="step"><h2><span class="num">5</span>Practice driving safely</h2>
<p>If your path requires supervised practice, track it. Practice city streets, lane changes, parking, backing, intersections, freeway/highway driving where appropriate, and driving in different conditions.</p>
<p>If you are under 18, Oregon has additional provisional-license and supervised-driving requirements. Check DMV for the current rules.</p></section>

<section class="step"><h2><span class="num">6</span>Schedule the drive test</h2>
<p>When you are ready, check DMV's current drive-test options and appointment rules. Make sure the vehicle you use is properly registered, insured and safe for the test.</p>
<div class="actions"><a href="https://www.oregon.gov/odot/dmv/pages/driverid/licenseget.aspx" target="_blank" rel="noopener">DMV License Steps ↗</a></div></section>

<section class="step"><h2><span class="num">8</span>Need help studying or getting to DMV?</h2>
<p>Use the Transportation directory for local transit, disability transportation and other options. Libraries can also be useful places to study or access computers.</p>

<section class="step"><h2>My First-License Checklist</h2>
<p>☐ I checked the current DMV requirements for my age and situation<br>
☐ I gathered my documents<br>
☐ I studied the Oregon Driver Manual<br>
☐ I completed the knowledge/permit steps that apply to me<br>
☐ I practiced driving<br>
☐ I checked the vehicle requirements<br>
☐ I scheduled/completed my drive test<br>
☐ I confirmed my license is valid before driving alone</p></section>

<p class="notice">General resource information, not legal advice. Oregon DMV requirements, fees, testing and document rules can change. Confirm current requirements directly with Oregon DMV.</p>
