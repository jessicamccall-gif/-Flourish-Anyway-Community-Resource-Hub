A simple starting path for getting an Oregon driver's license back in the Rogue Valley.

START HERE

First: find out what is actually blocking your license.

You can have more than one issue at the same time — court debt, DUII requirements, SR-22, treatment, ignition interlock, a waiting period, revocation or another DMV hold. Don't assume you have to pay everything at once.

1. Call Oregon DMV

Medford DMV: 987 Medford Center, Medford, OR 97504
Medford: 541-776-6025
Statewide: 503-945-5000

Ask exactly:
“What are ALL of the active suspensions, revocations, holds and reinstatement requirements preventing me from having a valid license?”

Also ask: “Am I eligible for a hardship permit?”

2. If it feels overwhelming, get local navigation help

Golden Rule ReEntry
715 W. Main St., Medford
541-930-8933

They provide peer/case-management help with DMV privilege restoration, transportation, resource navigation, goal setting and problem solving.

3. Check whether court debt or a court case is involved

Jackson County Circuit Court
100 S. Oakdale Ave., Medford
541-776-7171

Ask: “Do I have any cases, unpaid balances or court requirements that are preventing DMV reinstatement?”

If you have a current criminal Driving While Suspended case, ask the court whether you qualify for court-appointed counsel.

4. Ask if qualifying court debt can go on a reinstatement payment plan

Oregon License Reinstatement Program
877-222-2346 or 503-945-8199
oaa.help@dor.oregon.gov

Some qualifying Oregon Circuit Court debt may be eligible for a payment agreement. This does not remove every suspension or DUII-related requirement.

5. Ask about a hardship permit

Depending on your status, a hardship permit may allow limited driving for work, looking for work, medical care, addiction treatment and certain family needs.

Ask DMV about eligibility before paying a nonrefundable application fee.

6. If DUII treatment or documentation is involved

OnTrack Rogue Valley: 541-772-1777
Addictions Recovery Center: 541-779-1282

These may help when an assessment, treatment, documentation or reconnection with DUII services is part of reinstatement.

7. Transportation while you work on it

RVTD: 541-779-2877
RVTD TransLink: 541-842-2060

Useful for work, DMV, court and appointments. TransLink may cover eligible medical transportation.
